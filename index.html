About

Matt Moffett is an artist based in Tulsa, Oklahoma.

Matt Moffett has been an artist in Tulsa for nearly 30 years, but growing up he never thought that would be his future. “I always thought I was one of those people that didn’t have that gift,” Moffett says. It wasn’t until he had difficulties finding someone in Tulsa to paint a portrait of his dog that he decided to just do it himself. “Then all my friends were saying ‘paint my dog, paint my dog,’ and that’s how I started in art,” he says. Teaching college-level Spanish at the time, his newfound painting career was slow going at first. “My friend said, ‘Don’t quit your day job right away. Just work at it.’ I think the hardest part was giving myself permission to be an artist. But after a while, it just really took off,” Moffett explains, adding that he also transitioned into teaching art at elementary schools. In 2007 Moffett founded the Tulsa Girls Art School, a nonprofit afterschool art program in the Kendall Whittier neighborhood, where he worked as a teacher and the executive director for 10 years.
